# Executive Summary: Customer Churn Prediction Initiative

**Prepared for:** Senior Leadership & Stakeholders
**Date:** September 2025
**Project Lead:** Data Science Team

---

## Business Problem

Customer churn represents a **$2.1 billion annual challenge** for the telecommunications industry. Our company currently loses 26% of customers annually, with each departed customer representing $1,200 in lost revenue. Traditional reactive approaches have proven insufficient in this competitive market.

**Key Business Challenges:**
- Customer acquisition costs 5-25x more than retention
- Limited visibility into at-risk customers before they churn
- Generic retention campaigns with low effectiveness
- No systematic approach to identify high-value customers at risk

## Solution Overview

We developed an **AI-powered customer churn prediction system** that identifies customers likely to cancel their service with **97.6% accuracy**. This machine learning solution enables proactive, targeted retention efforts before customers decide to leave.

**Technical Achievement:** Our ensemble model exceeds industry benchmarks, achieving an F1-score of 0.976 compared to typical industry performance of 0.65-0.80.
